# Image return and later Phase 2 review

This is the review checklist prepared with the image handoff in Step 2.3. Step 2.4 has not been executed. No generated images or owner design choice exist yet.

For each returned image, record ID, direction, prompt revision, actual dimensions, local image file, SHA-256, generation information if available, observed deviations and owner feedback. Preserve originals. Do not mark a receipt received until its file actually exists and is inspected. Keep private reference material out of public images; these prompts need only synthetic content.

First compare A/B/C: readability, initial attention, resume/source/playback discoverability, density, comfort and overall tone. Record the chosen direction or explicit requested combination. Then generate the screen family with that reference. The source passage, product name and controls must remain consistent across images.

Screen review tasks after images are returned:

1. Find Resume, identify the source/course and describe where it returns.
2. Locate file, pasted-text and note import; inspect a failed/duplicate/interrupted item and its recovery.
3. Compare original/extracted text, identify the correction and see what Save affects.
4. Read a passage, find its original source, and distinguish your note from a tutor explanation.
5. Attempt a study question before feedback; inspect hint/reveal and saved-draft behavior.
6. Find listening controls and microphone start/stop; recover by typing after denial.
7. Write your own PQP response; confirm no generated outline/citation workflow appears.
8. Inspect prepared offline resources and a saved question while online tutoring is unavailable.
9. Find missing course rules, review progress without false mastery, and preview backup/restore.
10. Review the 390px image and desktop image at their intended sizes; check clipping, control targets, focus visibility and player overlap.
11. Decide whether the proposed visual-aid inspector has instructional value; revise or reject unnecessary 3D decoration.

Then perform Step 2.4's complete walkthrough and audit against screen-map.json, flows.json, component-states.json, tokens.json and the requirements register. Mark every required rendered-image check passed/failed with evidence; prompts cannot substitute. Pair static images with these interaction specifications and produce linked/reviewable designs or a dependency-free prototype if needed for the owner's flow review within Phase 2. Any simulated controls must be explicit.

Record each defect with candidate image/hash, observed problem, expected behavior and requested change. Repair and re-inspect within Phase 2. Successful image generation is not design acceptance. Only an actual owner acceptance record for the reviewed candidate closes Phase 2. Phase 3 needs its own explicit authorization.
