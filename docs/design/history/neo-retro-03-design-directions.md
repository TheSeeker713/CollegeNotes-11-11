# Neo Retro Glass — revised direction

Revision: neo-retro-glass-2. The owner rejected A/B/C and requested Neo Retroism, liquid glass and movable cards with convincing 3D-like shadows. This replaces Quiet Desk, Focus Studio and Editorial Notebook. Final appearance awaits generated images and user review.

## Appearance

Warm putty canvas, cream interiors, graphite text, deep violet/cobalt controls, small tangerine and mint instrument accents. Bold condensed retro headings, monospace numbered labels and clean 18px sans-serif prose. Chunky rounded silhouettes and substantial pill controls recall retro computers.

Each movable card has a thick liquid-glass shell: smoothly rounded refracting rims, visible thickness, bright upper-left bevels and subtle tinted edges. Background color fields blur and distort through exposed glass. Prose and input regions have solid cream backing inside the shell. Transparency remains visible around title bars and broad perimeter areas without making text depend on whatever sits behind it.

A fixed upper-left light gives every card a tight dark contact shadow and a wider soft lower-right cast shadow. Lifting a card weakens its contact shadow and makes the cast shadow larger, softer and more displaced. Shadows fall across lower cards as well as the canvas. This physical separation is the signature effect. These are visual targets; no renderer or physical simulation is implemented.

## Movable workspace

A compact fixed navigation rail accompanies independently movable Source, Notes, Study question and Listen cards. Source starts largest and central, Notes to the right, Listen below. Each card has an obvious grab handle, Pin control and named layout menu. Show Notes lifted over the edge of Study, with a grab cursor and subtle snap destination, leaving source prose clear.

Dragging starts only from the handle so editing, selection and audio seeking remain independent. Offer snap-to-grid or free placement, Pin/Unpin, Undo last move and Reset layout. Clamp cards to reachable bounds. Save position, size, stacking order and pin state per course. A failed save preserves the current working layout and text, and offers Retry. Fixed navigation keeps Undo and Reset reachable.

Keyboard: open Move menu and choose a destination, or enter move mode; arrows move, Enter places, Escape cancels. Announce placement once. Focus order remains Source → Notes/Tutor → Study → Listen regardless of visual coordinates. Bring a moved card forward without changing keyboard focus unexpectedly.

At narrow widths or high zoom, stack cards with Move up / Move down actions. Preserve the desktop arrangement for return to a wide window. Reduced motion removes tilt and animated lift while retaining placement and static shadows. Reduced transparency uses opaque cream shells and clear outlines. Both modes retain every function.
