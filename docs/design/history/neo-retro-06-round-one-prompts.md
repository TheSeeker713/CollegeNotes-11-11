## N01 — Movable liquid-glass study workspace

Create a polished front-on desktop app image at 1440×900, 8:5, filling the frame. Neo Retroism meets contemporary optical glass: warm putty #D8CDBB canvas, cream #FFFAF0 reading interiors, graphite #22232A text, deep violet/cobalt #4430A0 buttons, small tangerine and mint accents. Broad soft color fields behind cards make refraction visible. Use bold condensed retro headings, monospace numbered labels and clean readable sans-serif prose. Chunky rounded geometry, substantial pill buttons, precise instrument-like details and generous open space.

Arrange four independently movable glass cards: a large Source card centered slightly left, Notes upper right, a smaller Study question card below Notes, and a wide Listen card below Source. A compact fixed left rail shows CollegeNotes{11:11}, COMM 110, Home, Sources, Study, Practice, Requirements, Progress and Settings. Sources is active. A toolbar shows “Arrange”, “Undo move” and “Reset layout”. Every card has a clearly visible grab handle, numbered title and pin control.

Make the glass materially substantial: thick translucent shells, rounded refracting rims, bright upper-left bevels, subtly tinted side edges, visible thickness, and background blur/distortion through exposed glass. Prose and writing fields have solid cream inner surfaces framed by transparent glass. Preserve clearly visible glass around title bars and broad perimeter areas. Keep specular glare away from text.

CRITICAL SHADOW DIRECTION: use one coherent light from upper left. EVERY movable card casts a visible shadow onto the canvas or cards beneath it. Resting cards have a narrow dark contact shadow plus a broader soft lower-right cast shadow. Show Notes being lifted by its handle with a grab cursor. Its elevated state has clear separation from its shadow: weaker contact shadow, larger softer cast shadow displaced farther lower-right, falling onto the edge of the lower Study card. Source and Listen stay grounded with tighter shadows. Show a restrained outlined snap destination. Do not give every card the same generic flat drop shadow. Keep the overall screen face-on; the lifted card may tilt at most two degrees.

Source: “01 / SOURCE”, “Speaking with clear examples”, “Synthetic study sample · Section 2 of 6”, “View original”. Heading “Make an idea concrete”. Body at approximately 18px with generous line spacing: “A concrete example connects an unfamiliar idea to something an audience can picture. State the idea, give a specific example, and explain the connection.”

Notes: “02 / NOTES”, Notes and Tutor tabs, “Explain why the example supports the point.” and “Add note”.
Study: “03 / STUDY”, “What makes this example useful?” and violet “Check understanding”.
Listen: “04 / LISTEN”, play/pause, 03:18 / 12:40, seek, 1.0× and “Replay sentence”. Listening occupies its own card without covering prose.
Show small “Ready offline” and “Saved just now” labels as sample states.

Prioritize tangible glass, convincing height-dependent shadows, retro identity and readable content. No physical desk, room, hardware/device mockup, navigable world, ocean, people, invented grades or generated-outline/citation controls. Add discreet “Design concept · N01”.

## N02 — Resting, lifted, placed

Use N01 as reference. Create a 1800×1200 detail board with three large panels labeled “Resting”, “Lifted”, “Placed”. Show the SAME Notes card at the SAME scale over the same warm canvas and edge of a lower Study card. Keep light fixed at upper left.

Resting: thick liquid-glass rim, creamy readable note, tight contact shadow and close soft cast shadow.
Lifted: grab handle engaged, visible glass thickness, clear separation from shadow, weaker contact shadow and a much broader softer shadow farther lower-right, crossing the lower card. Include the destination outline.
Placed: Notes aligned to its new destination, contact shadow restored and cast shadow tightened.

Keep polished highlights, tinted refraction and the same retro typography, palette and controls throughout. Note text: “Explain why the example supports the point.” Beneath panels place “Handle to move”, “Shadow follows height”, “Snap or place freely”. Make glass borders and shadow differences large enough to inspect. This is a static interaction storyboard. No new theme or physical tabletop. Label “Design concept · N02”.
